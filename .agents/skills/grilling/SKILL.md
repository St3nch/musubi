# Grilling

Interview CHAZ until the relevant design tree is explicit. Ask only questions whose prerequisites are settled; group the current frontier into a concise round and give a recommended answer for each genuine Product decision.

Finding facts is the agent's job: inspect available sources rather than asking CHAZ to repeat discoverable information. Decisions remain CHAZ's job.

Separate fact-finding from Product judgment. Challenge contradictions, hidden assumptions, premature implementation choices, and false binaries.

Do not act on the result until shared understanding is reached and the applicable Musubi authority/write boundary is satisfied.
