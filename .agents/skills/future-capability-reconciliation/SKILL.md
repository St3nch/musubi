# Future capability reconciliation — Musubi mode

Use this method when a materially developed idea has reached the point where “later,” “maybe,” or “remember this” would be an unsafe ending. Idea origin is provenance, not authority.

## 1. Read before classifying

Read the smallest relevant live Musubi authority. If the idea depends on another VedaOps project, inspect that project's native authority read-only. Do not substitute chat memory, reviewer confidence, or research output for authority.

## 2. Teach, evaluate, recommend

Explain the idea, evaluate Product fit/evidence/timing/redundancy/authority impact, separate Product direction from implementation guesses, then recommend one disposition. Obtain CHAZ resolution when consequential.

## 3. Dispositions

- **Exploring** — understanding incomplete.
- **Rejected** — deliberately not part of the Product/approach.
- **Research Required** — important question lacks evidence.
- **Accepted Direction** — belongs in the Product if/when conditions are met; not backlog or implementation authority.
- **Promoted** — fresh CHAZ review allows entry into the normal current-work chain.
- **Superseded** — later evidence/direction replaces the earlier disposition.

## 4. Durable future items

Canonical register: `decisions/deferred.md`. For Accepted Direction or durable Research Required record Disposition, Product Pillar, Clock, Direction/Question, Why it matters, Why not now, Review trigger, Revisit point, Evidence basis, Cost of forgetting, and Not authorized.

A trigger creates review only. It never auto-promotes work. Strip premature provider, library, schema, model, or infrastructure choices unless separately settled.

## 5. Promotion

When a trigger fires or revisit point arrives, reassess current evidence. Only fresh CHAZ Product judgment can promote the item. Normal specification/ticket/review/external-effect gates still apply.

This skill is working method, not Product authority.
