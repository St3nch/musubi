# Musubi Deferred / Future Direction

Future capability is not backlog. A review trigger creates a fresh Product
review obligation only and never authorizes implementation.

## F1 — Idea Workbench

**Disposition:** Accepted Direction  
**Pillar:** Discovery & Ideas  
**Clock:** TRIGGERED

**Direction / question:**  
Allow CHAZ eventually to capture, develop, research, pressure-test, defer,
revive, reject, or mature ideas that do not yet belong to an existing Project.

**Why it matters:**  
Musubi may become uniquely useful when a new idea can be worked against existing
VedaOps projects, lessons, history, signals, constraints, and prior ideas.

**Why not now:**  
Core Musubi must first prove institutional-memory and cross-project reasoning
value.

**Review trigger:**  
Core Musubi is operating successfully and CHAZ begins regularly using Musubi to
preserve or develop ideas outside current Project boundaries.

**Revisit point:**  
First major Product review after the core Musubi prototype is in real use.

**Evidence basis:**  
CHAZ Product direction during Musubi pre-project design.

**Cost of forgetting:**  
Potential ideas and their rationale continue disappearing into temporary chat
history or get reconsidered without institutional context.

**Not authorized:**  
Idea scoring, automatic project creation, autonomous commissioning,
automatic implementation, or a speculative Idea Workbench platform.
