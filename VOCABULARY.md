# Vocabulary

This vocabulary is intentionally small. It exists to keep LLMs and humans from collapsing unlike things into one word.

## Project authority

The native source that owns a project's Product doctrine, accepted project decisions, implementation, code, project-local deferrals, runtime state where applicable, and project-local history.

Musubi may read project authority. It does not replace it.

## Institutional knowledge

Durable VedaOps-level understanding intentionally retained because it is useful beyond a single moment or project-local source.

## Signal

An observation that may matter but does not yet justify a durable conclusion or recommendation.

A signal can expire or prove irrelevant.

## Idea

A possibility worth retaining or exploring.

An idea is not a decision and is not authorization.

## Suggestion

A concrete recommendation for CHAZ / the Steward to consider.

A suggestion should identify why it matters now, its basis, and important limitations.

A suggestion is not project work.

## Lesson

Reusable institutional learning derived from experience.

A lesson should preserve enough context to show where it came from, when it applies, and where it may not transfer safely.

## Institutional decision

A VedaOps-level decision actually made by CHAZ whose natural scope is institutional or portfolio-wide rather than owned entirely by one project.

Musubi may store the durable record of such a decision. The LLM cannot create its authority by writing the record.

If an institutional decision changes an individual project's permitted work or doctrine, the consequence must enter that project's native authority through the normal governed process before it is treated there as project instruction.

## Candidate

A proposed Musubi addition or change that has not yet crossed the human review boundary into durable curated Musubi knowledge.

## Historical

Material intentionally preserved because it explains prior understanding, decisions, rejected ideas, or earlier conditions, but must not be mistaken for current truth.

## Unknown

Information that Musubi and available native authority cannot responsibly establish.

Unknown is a valid result.

## Musubi reasoner

The separate LLM/agent that reads Musubi and governed native authority, performs institutional reasoning, and surfaces possibilities.

Its project access is read-only.

## Read mode

A conversational mode in which the reasoner can read Musubi and authorized native sources but cannot mutate Musubi.

## Write mode

A mode in which the reasoner may prepare a proposed Musubi mutation. The exact proposed change must be shown to CHAZ before any durable Musubi write occurs.

## Critical distinctions

> **Retained does not mean believed.**

> **Believed does not mean decided.**

> **Suggested does not mean authorized.**

> **Trigger fired does not mean implementation authorized.**
