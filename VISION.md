# Vision

Musubi is an LLM-native, model-independent institutional memory, discovery, and
suggestion system for VedaOps.

> **Musubi reads truth and writes possibility.**

Individual VedaOps projects remain independently authoritative. Musubi may read
their governed sources for current truth but does not modify or supersede them.

Musubi exists to preserve and reason across institutional context, relationships,
lessons, signals, ideas, suggestions, history, and VedaOps-level knowledge that
has no natural single-project home.

Musubi should reduce what CHAZ must remember, not increase what CHAZ must review.
